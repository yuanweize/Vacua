# Vacua Brand Guidelines

## Philosophy & Identity
**Vacua** (Latin for *void*, *empty space*, *reclaimed volume*) is a safety-first storage intelligence engine for macOS.

The visual identity embodies:
- **Restraint**: Developer-tool aesthetics rooted in clarity, monochrome precision, and quiet authority.
- **Precision**: Crisp geometric apertures, negative space, and mathematical proportions.
- **Truthfulness**: Zero cliché icons (no brooms, trash cans, rocket ships, robot faces, or AI sparkles).

---

## Logo Geometry: The Aperture & Void
The Vacua mark consists of:
1. **The Outer Ring**: A circular boundary representing the storage perimeter and safety sandbox.
2. **The Convergent V**: Razor-sharp chevron strokes descending into the center, symbolizing precision and spatial reduction.
3. **The Core Void**: A central circular focal point representing reclaimed storage space.

### Scalability
- **16×16px / 32×32px**: Clean integer alignment ensures crisp rendering in terminal headers, menus, and tabs.
- **Scalable Vectors**: 100% path geometry, zero embedded raster, zero base64 blobs, zero commercial font dependencies.
